## Running a live Claude demo

Use the [pomegranate-demo skill](https://github.com/TimeGenius0/pomegranate/blob/master/skills/pomegranate-demo/SKILL.md) to provision a prospect's Product and Marketing workspaces from their website. The operator receives two private connector URLs; never put those URLs in this public index.

In Claude, enable one connector at a time and ask:

- Product: “What is our product strategy and who are our target customers?”
- Product: “What's on our product roadmap for the next six months, and why?”
- Marketing: “What's our go-to-market strategy and ideal customer profile?”
- Marketing: “What are the key messages and channels we are planning?”

The demo moment is grounded answers with citations, plus an honest “I don't know” when the workspace has no supporting context.

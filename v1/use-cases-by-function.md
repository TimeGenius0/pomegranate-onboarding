# Pomegranate use cases by function

Pomegranate keeps the current, answerable context a function would otherwise reconstruct from documents, threads, calls, and memory. Choose one or two recurring workflows first.

## Product

- Feature requests: collect, cluster, deduplicate, and triage requests.
- User insights: keep interview and call evidence available while drafting.
- PRDs and changes: track the current spec plus its decision history.
- Project status, dependencies, blockers, OKRs, and check-ins.

## Marketing and Go-to-market

- Social ideas, drafts, publishing schedule, and campaign notes.
- Campaign briefs and performance history.
- Prospecting and lightweight CRM for smaller teams.
- Brand voice and positioning checks before content ships.

## Sales

- Per-account call notes, pipeline, and follow-ups.
- Shared account history for handoffs.
- Objection handling and competitor responses grounded in calls.
- Won/lost reasons, pricing decisions, and discount approvals.

## Customer Support

- Known issues and current workarounds.
- Canonical answers and escalation paths.
- Ownership and recurring complaint themes for Product.

## Engineering and technical teams

- Architecture decisions and supersession history.
- Runbooks, incident postmortems, and shipped work.
- Onboarding context, conventions, standards, and PR reviews.

## Program and project management

- Cross-project status and dependency registers.
- Risks, issues, milestones, and date-change history.

## Operations and administration

- Vendor and contract registers, renewals, and terms.
- Procurement requests, approvals, accounts payable, and inventory.
- Internal policies and “how we do it” answers.

## Compliance, legal, and risk

- Current policy books and regulatory changes.
- Draft reviews against specific rules with citations.
- Obligations, deadlines, and audit context.

## Knowledge management

- Track important documents and keep the current version answerable.
- Give agents a cited reference layer over scattered drives and threads.

## Founders and leadership

- Strategy, positioning, decisions, reversals, goals, and rationale.
- The context agents need to draft consistently.
- Questions the team asks that the current context cannot answer.
